Treating a cell prevents future ignition and extinguishes it if it is burning. Neighbouring treated cells can form a barrier. Each panel follows its own policy on the same recorded scenario; an episode that ends earlier stays on its final frame.

What am I looking at?

Seed: the identifier controlling random choices in a recorded scenario. Selecting a seed here chooses an existing recording; it does not train or generate a new model.

Episode: one complete wildfire simulation. Interval: one policy decision followed by the configured number of simulator steps.

Neural checkpoint: saved learned network weights. The policy selects a cell from its current observation. A successful connected barrier must emerge from successive decisions.

Reward: the score after one decision. Total return: the sum of rewards in this recording. Higher is better. A single replay is an illustration, not proof that a policy performs best overall.
